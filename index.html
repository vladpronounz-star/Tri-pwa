How the mix works
Local videos and shared tab audio can enter TRI's speakers and recording mix. The Mix checkbox controls that route. YouTube embeds play independently and cannot be wired directly into TRI's recorder. On a supported desktop browser, open the video in its own tab, choose Share tab audio / video, select that tab and enable tab audio. Turn off the matching embed to avoid hearing it twice. Shared previews do not control the source tab.
Play All starts the loaded TRI layers and ready video players. Mobile browsers may require tapping each player once. YouTube needs internet and embedding permission. Classic browser PiP only allows one video at a time. TRI PiP Board uses Document Picture-in-Picture where supported: one always-on-top window containing several independently controlled video layers. This is the plural PiP mode. Multi-Float remains the in-TRI fallback. Classic single-video PiP is still offered for local videos. Hiding a local visual keeps its audio route; background and screen-off playback remain browser-dependent.
